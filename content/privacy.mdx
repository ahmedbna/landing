# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: January 1, 2026_

Orca ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Orca mobile application (the "App"). By using Orca, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide and improve Orca’s language learning experience.

### 1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use the App, we may collect the following personal information:

- **Name**
- **Email address**
- **Gender**
- **Birthday** (used to verify age eligibility and personalize learning)
- **Profile image**
- **Native language**
- **Learning language**

### 1.2 Voice and Speech Data

Orca uses **speech recognition** to help you practice pronunciation and speaking.

- Voice input is processed to evaluate pronunciation and provide feedback.
- Speech recognition is performed using on-device or platform-provided technologies where possible.
- We do **not** store raw voice recordings unless explicitly stated otherwise in the App.

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

- Device type and operating system
- App version
- Language preferences
- Anonymous usage data (e.g., feature usage, crashes)

This data is used strictly to improve performance, stability, and user experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your Orca account
- Personalize your language learning experience
- Match you with other students learning the same language
- Provide speaking, listening, and reading exercises
- Improve pronunciation and fluency feedback
- Communicate important updates or account-related messages
- Improve and optimize the App’s features and performance

We do **not** sell your personal data.

---

## 3. Text-to-Speech, Speech Recognition, and Audio

Orca uses the following technologies:

- **Speech Recognition** (via Expo / device APIs) to analyze spoken phrases
- **Offline on-device Text-to-Speech (Piper TTS)** to generate natural voice lessons
- **Background music** to enhance the learning experience

### Key Privacy Notes

- Text-to-speech is generated **on-device** and does not require sending your data to external servers.
- Background audio does not collect or process personal data.
- Speech recognition is used only for learning features and not for identification purposes.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

We do **not** share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited cases:

- **Service providers** that help operate the App (e.g., hosting, analytics), under strict confidentiality agreements
- **Legal requirements**, if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or property of users or others

All third parties are required to comply with applicable data protection laws.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

- Secure storage and encrypted connections where applicable
- Limited access to personal data
- Regular monitoring for security vulnerabilities

Despite our efforts, no system is 100% secure. You use Orca at your own risk.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to:

- Provide the App’s services

- Resolve disputes
- Enforce our agreements

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Object to or restrict certain processing

You can manage most of your information directly within the App.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without proper consent, we will delete it promptly.
